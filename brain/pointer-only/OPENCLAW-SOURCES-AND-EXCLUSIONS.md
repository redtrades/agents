## GovCon exclusions

The capture intentionally excludes code, workflows, recipes, runtime directories,
raw research data, attachments, generated proposals/deliverables, samples, run
artifacts, brand/customer files, and all case material. Three otherwise narrative
research documents were also rejected after an identifier-pattern screen found
probable actual firm identifiers. Seventeen council documents were conservatively
rejected because a case-or-firm identifier screen matched them.
