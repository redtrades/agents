# MARKETING — many doors, one factory

2026-08-23. Companion to `sop/PLAN-V5.md`. If you are writing an email, a page, a newsletter issue, or a LinkedIn draft, you are here.

The factory makes industry reports and opportunity packets. This file is how people find them. **The paid object is always the packet** (their awards × this notice). Everything free exists to put the right person on the list so we can sell the next packet.

---

## The system

```
LinkedIn / partners / public NAICS pages / communities
                    ↓
           LANDING PAGE
     free industry report · UEI lookup · newsletter
                    ↓
              NEWSLETTER
           (codes they picked)
                    ↓
        click a live notice
                    ↓
           PACKET  $699
                    ↓
            CODE WATCH
```

Matched outbound on a live notice is a **second on-ramp**. It does not replace the hub. A buyer from either door lands on the newsletter.

---

## Door 1 — Landing page + UEI lookup

The hub. Stand this up before a content program.

**On the page**

- One sentence: reports on your market, plus a draft mapped to your public record. Veteran-owned.
- **UEI box.** Submit → pull awards → show 0–3 open notices that score list-3. Teaser: one requirement row + one of their PIIDs. CTA: unlock the packet ($699) or email me the PDF.
- **Free industry report** download (one NAICS to start, 236220 or 541519). Email + NAICS required. UEI optional.
- Newsletter signup (checkboxes for codes).
- Sample page (redacted map). Not a full unpaid packet.

Agents generate the UEI result and the report. Mike approves the page copy once. After that, data refreshes do not need new prose approval unless a number on a public page is new (same provenance rule as a deliverable). A wrong public number is a reputation gate fail.

---

## Door 2 — Newsletter (storefront)

Weekly. Not an essay.

Per code they checked:

- 3 numbers: open now, 90-day flow, median offers on comparable awards
- 1 live notice line (title, agency, close date)
- Two links: full industry report · packet for that notice (if we built list-3 packets)

No packet link for list-1 firms. If we only have the industry report, that is the only button.

Buttondown, markdown in, Mike clicks send. Agents draft. First issue ships only for codes that already have a real report.

---

## Door 3 — Matched outbound

For list-3 firms on a notice with 10–20 days left. Packet already exists.

Template: `templates/outreach/email-packet.md`.

Rules:

- One PIID in sentence one or do not send.
- One CTA: want the packet.
- 3-touch max, done before the deadline (see `templates/outreach/` cadence).
- Different template for newly certified (no awards) and for consultants. Do not reuse this one.

Volume comes from **more notices**, not a bigger dump per notice. A healthy week looks like 4–8 notices × 8–15 firms, not 1 notice × 2,000 listed codes.

---

## Door 4 — LinkedIn

Two posts a week. Agents draft data + a first cut. Mike rewrites and posts. No auto-post.

Formats that fit the factory:

- Weekly counts (“N open in these codes; M close in 14 days”)
- One redacted map row
- One teardown (“type says Sources Sought; paragraph 4 says MAC only”)

Every post links the UEI page or the signup. Comments on other people's posts are Mike-only.

---

## Door 5 — Free industry report as magnet

Give away a real 6–8 page cited report. Not a 2-page pamphlet.

Who it is for: APEX counselors (they can share free things), landing-page SEO, newsletter signup, partner intros.

Paid vs free: free is the **market**. Paid is **their draft on a live notice**. Same PDF twice is not a business.

---

## Door 6 — Partners

Low heat. Agents prep the one-pager and the list. Mike sends.

| Partner | Give | Get |
|---|---|---|
| APEX / PTAC counselors | Free report + newsletter | Warm credibility |
| VetCert / registration shops | “First 90 days in your codes” briefing | Newly certified names |
| Bonding agents, GovCon CPAs | NAICS report their clients will read | Intros |
| Proposal consultants | White-label packets or overflow | $249/mo feed test, or referrals |
| Prime small-business liaisons | “Who in this NAICS has actually done this” | A second buyer on the same data |

Do not pitch counselors. Hand them free assets.

## Door 7 — Rooms they already use

r/GovernmentContracting, veteran FB groups, APEX webinars, VA DAP, NVSBE (2026-12-08–09, Cleveland — the event is on; it is not “gone”). Podcasts that already cover Sources Sought.

Answer questions. Link the free report or the UEI page. Do not drop a $699 pitch in a thread.

Guesting: agents write a 1-page brief of live numbers. Mike talks for 30 minutes.

---

## Door 8 — Public NAICS pages (later)

One URL per code, regenerated from the same data as the industry report. CTA = newsletter + UEI. Trigger: daily ingest is stable and the first packets have sold. Not week 1.

---

## Door 9 — Code watch (after first paid packet)

“Email me when a notice in this code matches this UEI.” That *is* the subscription, without inventing Core. When it fires, the packet is the bill.

## Copy rules

- We already built it / here is the map / every number has an award ID.
- We do not claim win rates.
- We do not call the draft a winning proposal.
- We do not say we are the only finished document under $500. AI tools draft. We verify, map, and deliver.
- Veteran-owned is a door opener, not the pitch.

Objection (“why not SamSearch?”): `templates/outreach/objection-ai-saas.md`.

## First 30 days (marketing only)

1. Page up: UEI + one free report + signup.
2. One matched batch sent.
3. Newsletter issue 1.
4. Two LinkedIn posts.
5. One APEX counselor email with the free report. No ask.

If the matched batch produces a paid packet, keep every door. If it produces nothing, change the **packet**, not the number of channels.
