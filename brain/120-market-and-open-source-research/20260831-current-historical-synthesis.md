# Current–historical synthesis

## Current owner decisions

Prefer composable adoption: use maintained open source or low-cost services for commodity responsibilities and build only differentiated contracts and domain behavior that remain missing. Material infrastructure remains subject to real-workflow bakeoffs. The archive preserves citations, revisions, supported claims, and local analysis; it does not deploy or select the researched products.

## Historical evidence worth retaining

The market research supplies candidate categories—agent runtimes, workflow engines, sandboxes, gateways, evaluation, observability, declarative infrastructure, and managed substrates—plus dated limits, licensing, and operational cautions. The GovCon prior-art catalog is especially useful for a different reason: it separates commercial-code borrowing permitted by license from reference-only learning and records concrete gaps where native work would be necessary.

## How to use it

Treat each report as a source-indexed hypothesis. Revalidate freshness, license, security posture, deployment suitability, economics, and fit against a current accepted workflow before any trial. “Adopt,” “borrow,” “skip,” and similar labels inside historical reports are the report authors’ dated dispositions, not archive decisions.
