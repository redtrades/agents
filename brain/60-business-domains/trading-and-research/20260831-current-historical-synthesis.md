# Current historical synthesis

## Historical research thesis

The captured trading research recommends a read-only, free-tier-first research loop: store market-data pulls, validate findings with deterministic computation, produce a nightly brief, and use prediction markets as an information signal rather than an automated trading venue. It states a categorical no-auto-trading line: no placing, cancelling, or modifying orders without a separate explicit written approval.

## Durable safety and evidence principles

- Numeric market outputs must come from deterministic code and recorded data, not language-model arithmetic.
- Treat data entitlement, market-access, fees, regulatory status, and source freshness as changing facts requiring re-verification before use.
- An LLM may organize inputs, enforce checklists, and narrate documented calculations; it may not invent probabilities, override limits, size positions, or execute trades.
- Keep every candidate, assumption, source timestamp, and result traceable.
