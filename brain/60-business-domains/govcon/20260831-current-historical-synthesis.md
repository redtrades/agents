# Current historical synthesis

## Historical business thesis

GovCon Factory was defined as a federal-only factory that turns public solicitation data into buyer-actionable proposal deliverables. The current niche was SDVOSB, but the architecture was intended to be buyer-agnostic: a NAICS is configuration, a solicitation type adds templates and gates, and agency format is rendering. The on-demand path was the stated purchase trigger; continuous scanning supplied the opportunity funnel.

## Durable domain lessons

- The product is a deliverable, not a data feed, dashboard, or subscription.
- Public facts may ground starter material, but company-specific facts must be supplied by the customer. No invented past performance.
- A useful opportunity contains a specific checkable fact about a specific firm, not merely a broad lead list.
- Preserve provenance, evidence, and human review. They are quality hygiene, not an asserted differentiator.
- Staged transforms and fail-closed checks are useful historical patterns. They do not prove a complete factory existed.

## Current reboot position

GovCon opportunity and proposal operations are in the initial validation portfolio. The current archive decision does not authorize marketing, outreach, proposal submission, external delivery, or revival of the legacy implementation.
