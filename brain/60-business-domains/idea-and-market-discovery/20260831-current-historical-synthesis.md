# Current historical synthesis

## Current reboot decision

Business ideas and market signals are valid experiment types. Agents may discover bounded, low-risk experiments only with an explicit hypothesis, evidence, metrics, budget, and stopping rule. Results propose implications; only validated learning is promoted.

## Historical research synthesis

The idea-factory research separates automatable, free, low-risk discovery from constrained or gray sources that must be explicitly decided rather than quietly adopted. It maps a validation loop from sourcing through evidence review to distribution, and documents why fully automated attempts fail: weak evidence, no real buyer validation, compliance drift, and output volume mistaken for demand.

## Domain contract

- Gather hypotheses from lawful, permitted sources.
- Record source evidence, buyer/problem statement, alternatives, and falsifiers.
- Test with bounded experiments before building a company or campaign.
- Keep sales, outreach, spending, and external publishing outside autonomous discovery unless separately authorized.
- Promote a validated pattern into knowledge, not directly into an operating commitment.
