# Current historical synthesis

## Current reboot decision

Build a private knowledge base before rebuilding anything. Its co-equal outcomes are a personal operator OS and a declarative company factory. Validate shared capabilities through paired vertical slices, not an abstract platform. The operator retains approval over consequential action; learning changes institutional knowledge only after governed validation.

## Historical era-canon

OpenClaw framed the earlier system as a personal force multiplier first, a product path second: turn research, meetings, code intent, and attention into durable action while keeping the human in command. The recovered whole-estate vision broadened that into a solo-operator enterprise OS that can define, operate, evaluate, improve, and retire agent-run companies and projects.

## What survives the reboot

- Operator attention, durable knowledge, deliverable quality, model and harness replaceability, bounded autonomy, and evidence-backed learning are enduring intent.
- A company is a domain outcome with its own data, rules, outputs, and controlled experiments, not merely a dashboard or agent conversation.
- Infrastructure candidates, runtime configuration, and past delivery machinery are historical evidence only. This pack does not select or implement them.
