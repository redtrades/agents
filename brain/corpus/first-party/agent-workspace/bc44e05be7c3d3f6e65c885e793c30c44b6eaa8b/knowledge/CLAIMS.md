# Claims

Sourced facts worth remembering across a change of harness or model. See
constitution rule 2: every claim below carries a `source:`, or it gets
deleted rather than hedged.

Format:

```
- claim: "the claim, stated plainly"
  source: "file path, URL, or commit hash"
```

- claim: "A prior retrospective on this workspace found 65 codified rules with exactly one of them actually enforced."
  source: "conversation with Mike, 2026-08-15, cited verbatim when this workspace was designed"
- claim: "Before this workspace existed, two folders one letter apart existed with the wrong one at the canonical name, an agent wrote to a folder that had been renamed out from under it, and a document asserted it had been re-synced when it had not."
  source: "conversation with Mike, 2026-08-15 — the evidence cited for building this workspace"
