# Resolved owner questions

Current intent decisions 43 and 52 resolve the owner-level questions formerly listed here:

1. Isolated prompts, skills, workflows, routing, retrieval, and role variants may change during experiments. Policy, permissions, budgets, canon, evaluation criteria, production state, and promotion authority are outside the mutable surface.
2. Every class uses fixed and held-out tests, baseline-relative material improvement, no material regression, and exact-candidate review; medium and high risk add progressively stronger repeatability and independence.
3. Independence is risk-tiered: separate blinded review for low risk, differently configured or different-model evidence for medium risk, and human plus independent-model evidence for high risk.
4. Owner-set caps, independent portfolio reallocation, adaptive exploratory runs, frozen confirmatory runs, tiered retention, and multi-objective domain-weighted thresholds govern the portfolio.
