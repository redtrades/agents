# Decisions and conflicts

## Current decisions

- Self-improvement uses isolated variants, fixed and held-out evaluation, independent selection, and governed promotion.
- The experiment abstraction includes technical and nontechnical operational hypotheses.
- Validated learning is promoted into institutional knowledge deliberately, rather than changing the canon automatically.
- Promotion evidence and evaluator independence are risk-tiered. Candidates cannot modify policy, evaluators, production state, or their own promotion authority.
- Portfolio caps, adaptive exploratory work, frozen confirmatory runs, independent reallocation, and multi-objective domain-weighted evaluation govern experiments.
