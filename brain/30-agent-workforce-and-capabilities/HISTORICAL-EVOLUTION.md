# Historical evolution

1. **OpenClaw:** articulated a harness-agnostic baseline workforce, specialist blueprints, model routing, and action gating.
2. **agent-workspace and agent-configs:** moved attention to cross-harness continuity, skills, issue/worktree discipline, and an explicit account of instruction drift.
3. **agent-mesh:** distilled the durable intent: adapters should not become authority, and evidence-bearing workflow artifacts must outlive sessions.
4. **Current archive decision:** preserves the workforce and capability concepts while refusing to make any past runtime, configuration, or fixed topology a future-stack decision.
