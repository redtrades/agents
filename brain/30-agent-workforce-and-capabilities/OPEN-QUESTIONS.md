# Resolved owner questions

Current intent decisions 44-45 resolve the owner-level questions formerly listed here:

1. Capability metadata is tiered: every discovery record includes name, purpose, owner, version/source, allowed scopes, compatible runtimes, required permissions, expected cost, freshness, and verification status; promoted or consequential use adds the full dependency, risk, evaluation, failure, rollback, and deprecation record.
2. Optional functional role blueprints are shared; specialized data, workflows, deliverables, and role names remain domain extensions.
3. Runtime-neutral contracts and selectively shared, behaviorally verified skills use thin native adapters. Presence or syntax compatibility alone is insufficient.
4. Historical artifacts may enter a quarantined candidate library. Evaluation and adoption require a named gap and comparison with current alternatives.
