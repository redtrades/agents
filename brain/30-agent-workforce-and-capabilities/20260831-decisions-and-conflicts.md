# Decisions and conflicts

## Current decisions

- Infrastructure, capabilities, workflow artifacts, knowledge, and policy are separate categories.
- A lightweight scoped capability catalog is preferred over infrastructure-level ceremony for every skill or tool trial.
- Human approval remains required for consequential, destructive, spending, security, and promotion effects.
- The shared organizational contract covers goals, work, actors, capabilities, artifacts, evidence, decisions, experiments, outcomes, authority, budgets, and provenance. Optional functional role blueprints are shared; domain roles and data remain typed extensions.
- Runtime-neutral contracts and behaviorally verified shared skills use thin native adapters. Capability metadata is lightweight for discovery and expands before broad or consequential promotion.
- Historical capabilities may enter a quarantined candidate library; live evaluation requires a named gap and current-alternative comparison.
