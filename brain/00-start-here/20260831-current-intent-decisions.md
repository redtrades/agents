# Current intent decisions from the archive reboot

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: current owner answers captured during the historical-consolidation reboot

This file preserves the decisions made while defining the archive and recovering
the larger system intent. It is not a substitute for the historical evidence.
When a source disagrees with this file, record the disagreement and ask the owner
whether the historical decision is superseded, still valid in a narrower scope,
or was misunderstood.

## Knowledge and repository boundaries

1. Use a compact canon plus deeper domain books.
2. The personal agent operating system and autonomous company factory are
   co-equal outcomes with substantial shared capabilities.
3. Validate reuse through paired or portfolio vertical slices rather than by
   building an abstract platform first.
4. Use a hybrid knowledge structure: living normalized syntheses and indexes,
   self-contained reading folders, and source-preserving original documents.
5. Capture every intent-bearing artifact and referenced key research. Inventory
   irrelevant code, generated data, forks, and runtime material without copying
   them wholesale.
6. `agent-knowledge-archive` is the permanently private workspace for this
   reboot and historical consolidation.
7. Do not continue this reboot inside `agent-platform`. Its existing rules,
   compliance machinery, lifecycle stages, and code are historical evidence for
   this effort, not instructions governing the archive work.
8. Decide later whether the future platform implementation belongs in a cleaned
   existing repository or a new successor. Existing code receives no authority
   merely because it already exists.
9. Resolve historical conflicts formally. Current explicit owner decisions
   outrank prior syntheses; verified evidence and first principles inform the
   decision; newer files do not automatically win.

## System intent

10. Use bounded autonomy: reversible internal work may proceed autonomously;
    consequential external, destructive, security, or spending effects require
    appropriate grants or approval.
11. Self-improvement uses isolated variants, fixed and held-out evaluation,
    independent selection, and governed promotion.
12. The experiment abstraction is broader than swarm mutation. It also covers
    software methods, business ideas, operations, research, market signals,
    GovCon proposals, prompts, and workflows.
13. Maintain a governed experiment portfolio. Agents discover opportunities and
    may admit bounded low-risk experiments with hypotheses, evidence, metrics,
    budgets, and stopping rules.
14. Experimental results append evidence and proposed implications. A candidate
    cannot rewrite the canon or promote itself. Validated learning moves through
    the separate governed promotion boundary; decision 51 permits bounded
    automation only inside explicit grants and independent thresholds.
15. Define agents, swarms, projects, and companies declaratively where useful,
    with imperative orchestration or business-process code where configuration
    is insufficient.
16. Share a small stable organizational core and add typed domain extensions.
17. Prefer composable adoption: use maintained open source or low-cost services
    for commodity responsibilities and build only the differentiated contracts
    and domain behavior that remain missing.
18. Follow modular systems-engineering principles and preserve replaceable seams.
19. Use a policy-driven hybrid deployment posture across local, self-hosted,
    free-tier, subscription, and managed services.

## Knowledge, memory, and proactivity

20. Maintain a global institutional memory pool that seeds agents and learns
    from validated discoveries, with scoped personal, project, and company
    overlays.
21. Reusable research, principles, patterns, skills, evaluations, and sanitized
    lessons enter the global pool by default. Raw sensitive or organization-owned
    information remains scoped unless deliberately promoted.
22. Exact source artifacts and structured records remain authoritative, while
    semantic memory and knowledge graphs provide the active working interface.
    Semantic entries retain provenance, freshness, confidence, and scope.
23. Preserve decision narratives and linked evidence, not mandatory replay of
    full transcripts. Historical reasoning is an escalation path; agents should
    normally read current material.
24. A future living intelligence system should ingest permitted news, research,
    market information, and emerging patterns; classify and connect them through
    wiki or graph relationships; and proactively surface implications.
25. Git should hold the governed control and promoted knowledge layer. A future
    replaceable knowledge service may hold the larger corpus, indexes, embeddings,
    graph relationships, and ingestion state.
26. Proactivity uses declarative interests and monitoring plus a bounded open
    discovery budget.
27. Free-tier and small local models may perform high-volume classification,
    extraction, monitoring, and candidate generation, with stronger models used
    when quality or consequence requires them.

## Infrastructure, workflows, and capabilities

28. Select orchestration infrastructure through capability-driven bakeoffs using
    real workflows. Start with one primary backbone; allow specialized engines
    later when proven workflows or businesses materially require them.
29. Adopt or replace material infrastructure only when evidence shows material
    net improvement after migration and operating cost.
30. Keep infrastructure separate from capabilities and experiments:
    - infrastructure includes runtimes, orchestration engines, workflow execution
      systems, storage, identity, networking, observability, sandboxes, and
      deployment;
    - capabilities include skills, plugins, MCP servers and tools, models, and
      other reusable abilities;
    - experiments are hypothesis-and-evidence units that use infrastructure and
      capabilities;
    - knowledge includes sources, memory, evidence, decisions, and learning;
    - policy defines authority, permissions, budgets, and promotion rules.
31. Workflow engines and workflow execution systems are infrastructure.
    Individual workflows and business processes are versioned operational
    artifacts.
32. Use a lightweight shared capability catalog with scoped availability. Do not
    impose infrastructure-replacement ceremony on discovering or trying a skill,
    plugin, MCP server, prompt, or workflow.

## Archive-specific decisions

36. The repository is named `agent-knowledge-archive` and remains private.
37. Use the approved hybrid layout: topic reading packs plus a source-preserving
    corpus and central manifests.
38. Preserve current versions plus meaningful milestone versions rather than
    every ordinary Git revision.
39. Use a tiered sensitive boundary. Never copy live secrets. Keep necessary
    sensitive evidence in a restricted annex and keep case material outside.
40. For external research, preserve citations, revisions, supported claims,
    short compliant excerpts, and local analysis. Copy complete external
    documents only when appropriate for their license and archive purpose.
41. DocOps, CI enforcement, SDLC policy implementation, orchestration selection,
    and platform rebuilding are outside the scope of this archive task.
42. Use tiered retention for provenance-linked operational evidence: retain
    canonical and source artifacts; expire or compact raw logs according to
    sensitivity, value, and legal need; keep derived claims linked to retained
    sources; and deliberately promote reusable sanitized knowledge to the
    global pool. Domain-specific policy overlays set the applicable details.

## Owner interview decisions recorded 2026-08-31

43. Agents may create and test isolated variants of prompts, skills, workflows,
    routing, retrieval, and role definitions. Candidates cannot alter policy,
    permissions, budgets, canonical knowledge, evaluation criteria, production
    state, or their own promotion authority. Promotion evidence is risk-tiered:
    every class requires fixed and held-out tests, meaningful baseline-relative
    improvement, no material regression, and exact-candidate review by a blinded
    non-generator; medium-risk work adds repeated results and a differently
    configured or different-model evaluator; high-risk work requires human review
    plus independent-model evidence. A candidate may propose promotion but cannot
    promote itself.
44. The shared organizational core is an implementation-neutral contract for
    goals, work items, actors, capabilities, artifacts, evidence, decisions,
    experiments, measurable outcomes, authority, budgets, and provenance.
    Domains own specialized data, workflows, deliverables, and role names. The
    shared role layer provides optional owner, coordinator, researcher/planner,
    executor, reviewer, and knowledge-custodian blueprints rather than a fixed
    roster.
45. Portability means runtime-neutral contracts plus selectively shared skills
    and prompts where compatibility is behaviorally verified. Thin native
    adapters handle runtime differences; filesystem presence or syntax
    compatibility alone is not portability evidence. Capability metadata is
    tiered: every discovery record names the capability, purpose, owner,
    version/source, allowed scopes, compatible runtimes, required permissions,
    expected cost, freshness, and verification status. Broadly promoted or
    consequential capabilities also require dependencies, inputs and outputs,
    risks, alternatives, evaluation receipts, known failures, rollback, and
    deprecation status. Promising historical artifacts may enter a quarantined
    candidate library, but evaluation and adoption require a named current gap
    and comparison with current alternatives.
46. The personal operator OS integrates decision support, proactive intelligence,
    institutional memory, and bounded execution. Conversation is the primary
    control surface; proactive briefs and approvals use bounded inboxes; durable
    artifacts remain authoritative; and a command center is optional visibility,
    not a queue the operator must maintain.
47. Proactivity is digest-first with domain-specific urgency, confidence, quiet
    hours, budget, and delivery overlays. Intelligence uses a layered deliverable:
    urgent alerts when warranted, a concise evidence-linked decision brief by
    default, and an expandable research dossier for deeper analysis.
48. Every action produces a receipt recording actor, purpose, scope, target,
    authority source, timestamps, inputs, outputs, verification, and budget where
    applicable. Read-only and disposable analysis stops at that universal record;
    reversible internal changes additionally require a standing scoped grant and
    result receipt; consequential external, destructive, security, spending, or
    promotion effects require exact approval or an explicit bounded grant, named
    targets and budgets, expiry and idempotency where applicable, and verified
    outcome evidence.
49. Orchestration selection begins provisionally with the simplest maintained
    candidate, then requires a common intake, plan, execute, verify, review,
    approve, and promote lifecycle; realistic domain-native trials; and a
    cross-domain bakeoff covering at least two materially different workflows.
    Evidence, rather than provisional convenience, determines retention or
    replacement.
50. Market research, software evaluation, business evidence, and operational
    experiments may keep separate domain evidence schemas. They interoperate at
    the artifact, provenance, authority, and receipt boundaries rather than
    through one mandatory normalized claim schema.
51. Scoped domain writers maintain local derived knowledge. The global pool has
    a single promotion boundary with source and append-only evidence links.
    Delegated, revocable curators and independent generator, reviewer, and curator
    separation govern promotion. Sanitized low-risk entries may promote
    automatically only inside an explicit grant after predetermined provenance,
    validation, freshness, and reuse thresholds pass; sensitive, disputed,
    policy-changing, or high-impact entries escalate to the owner. A newer
    derived entry replaces the current view while prior versions, sources, and
    supersession history remain available.
52. Experiment governance is hybrid: the owner sets global and domain caps;
    agents may admit bounded low-risk experiments; and an independent portfolio
    manager may stop or reallocate work within those caps. New spending,
    increased authority, or higher-risk work returns to the owner. Exploratory
    runs may adapt with complete change logs and independent oversight;
    confirmatory runs freeze criteria before held-out evaluation, and a later
    change returns the run to exploratory status. Improvement must exceed baseline
    noise, cover quality, cost, latency, reliability, risk, operator attention,
    and reversibility, and respect domain-specific weights and hard regression
    limits.
53. GovCon remains a validation domain, but its customer, wedge, scope, pricing,
    and purchase trigger are reopened. Only the durable principles survive by
    default: buyer-actionable deliverables, no invented company facts, traceable
    evidence, and human review. Trading and market research are parked and do not
    shape the current portfolio or architecture until the owner reopens them.
54. The autonomous software factory's long-term outcome is an engineering
    institution that combines governed delivery, product creation, reusable
    capabilities, institutional learning, portfolio management, and retirement
    of failed products. Consequential effects remain human- or grant-controlled.
55. A declarative company is both an organizational blueprint and a stateful
    operating unit. Economic authority is semi-autonomous, capability-specific,
    and risk-tiered; it is never inherent in the declaration. The standard tiers
    are: Tier 0 observe; Tier 1 reversible internal work; Tier 2 prepare external
    effects for approval; Tier 3 execute specifically granted low-to-moderate-risk
    external effects within named bounds; and Tier 4 require case-specific human
    approval for legal commitments, material spending, security changes,
    destructive actions, sensitive disclosures, personnel actions, and major
    promotions. A declared tier is a ceiling; narrower grants determine actual
    authority.
56. Within owner-set caps, the company factory may incubate, fund, evaluate,
    pause, and retire bounded company experiments. Scaling, legal formation,
    material spending, and consequential commitments return to the owner.
    Validation is staged: evidenced buyer problem before experimentation,
    observable buyer behavior before external incubation, commercial commitment
    before scaling, and repeatability before durable company promotion. Normal
    stopping uses predeclared limits and portfolio opportunity cost; authority,
    evidence, sensitive-data, or uncontrolled-effect failures pause immediately;
    a time-bounded recovery plan is allowed only while the hypothesis remains
    credible.
57. Archive and strategy steady state is bounded and decision-ready: provenance
    and safety checks pass; material conflicts are decided or explicitly deferred;
    current intent is coherent; and the shared contracts and acceptance criteria
    for paired vertical-slice trials are defined. Infrastructure, implementation
    repository, and architecture selection remain separate, evidence-based,
    owner-approved work.
58. The first implementation target is the AISDLC: the system that builds the
    system. Begin with a bounded Fusion adoption trial and run Paperclip as a
    mutually exclusive challenger against the same neutral lifecycle contract.
    `agent-sdlc` begins as a composition and assurance repository rather than a
    second controller. Promptfoo and Inspect AI provide the initial evaluation
    foundation. The approved architecture and plan are recorded in
    `20260831-aisdlc-architecture-decision.md` and
    `20260831-aisdlc-mvp-implementation-plan.md`.

## Working method for this reboot

- Use parallel subagents for independent discovery and synthesis lanes.
- Use isolated worktrees and non-overlapping path ownership for mutations.
- Route mechanical inventory, copying, hashing, and formatting to efficient
  models; reserve stronger models for reconciliation and final review.
- Keep reports file-based and return only compact status and decisions to the
  coordinating session.
- As new evidence clarifies intent, incorporate it. When evidence exposes a
  material conflict or lost nuance, ask one source-backed question at a time.
