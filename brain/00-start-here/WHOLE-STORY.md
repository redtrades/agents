# Whole story

## Current reboot

This private archive exists to recover intent before any rebuild. Its current
owner decisions make the personal operator OS and declarative company factory
co-equal outcomes; shared capabilities must be tested through paired or
portfolio vertical slices, not an abstract platform first. Decision 58 later
selects the AISDLC as the first implementation target; long-term lifecycle
authority and downstream portfolio sequencing remain evidence-gated.

`agent-platform` is important source evidence: it preserves proposals,
implementation attempts, controls, and failures. Its rules, lifecycle stages,
and code do **not** govern this archive or authorize a rebuild. The same
non-activation boundary applies to preserved OpenClaw material, prompts,
runtime configuration, and historical stack recommendations.

## Historical arc

- **OpenClaw era:** personal-force-multiplier intent, bounded roles, durable
  knowledge, and early architecture and failure analysis.
- **Estate reconstruction era:** the intent widened to a solo-operator system
  that can support the operator and define, operate, evaluate, improve, and
  retire domain companies.
- **Reboot era (from 2026-08-31):** the archive is the private consolidation
  record; explicit owner decisions outrank historical syntheses. The first
  implementation direction is now the approved AISDLC adoption and assurance
  trial. Its long-term lifecycle authority and downstream infrastructure,
  runtime, and memory-service choices remain evidence-gated decisions.
