# Current–historical synthesis

## Current owner decisions

Bounded autonomy permits reversible internal work while consequential external, destructive, security, or spending effects require appropriate approval. Experiments append evidence and proposed implications; institutional knowledge promotion is deliberate. Policy defines authority, permissions, budgets, and promotion rules and is distinct from every other category.

## Historical evidence worth retaining

The platform operating model specifies a useful historical vocabulary: effect classification, exact target/scope/revision, fail-closed admission, independent review, expected-head promotion, and receipts. The runtime audit provides a critical counterweight: file presence, a generated projection, or a registered hook does not prove runtime activation or behavioral enforcement. Activation must be evidenced per runtime and profile.

## Stable boundary

Prompts, copied instructions, and role labels are soft evidence. Hard safety or authority claims require behaviorally verified controls and exact receipts. A workflow engine, provider, harness, issue tracker, chat thread, memory store, or dashboard must not silently become a competing authority.
