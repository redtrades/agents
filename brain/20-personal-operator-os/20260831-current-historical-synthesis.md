# Current historical synthesis

## Current reboot decision

The personal operator OS is a co-equal outcome, not a side effect of company automation. It integrates decision support, proactive intelligence, institutional memory, and bounded execution. Conversation is the primary control surface; bounded inboxes carry proactive briefs and approvals; durable artifacts remain authoritative; and a command center supplies optional visibility rather than mandatory queue maintenance.

## Historical era-canon

OpenClaw's primary job was to compound one operator's attention, memory, and decision velocity. Its concrete early jobs were research and a vault, coding support, and self-improvement in service of those outcomes. Proactive-agent research adds the essential constraint: surface only items whose expected value beats interruption cost, with deduplication, continuity, and budget gates.

## Enduring operating principles

- Convert intake into traceable knowledge and concise decisions, not activity dashboards.
- Keep raw sensitive or organization-owned information scoped; semantic memory is an active interface, not source authority.
- Favor an operator who directs goals, budgets, policy, and exceptions over a system that demands queue maintenance.
- Use autonomous internal work only inside explicit boundaries. External, destructive, security, and spending effects remain approval-sensitive.
- Deliver intelligence as an urgent alert when warranted, a concise decision brief by default, and an expandable research dossier when deeper evidence is needed.
