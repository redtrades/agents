# Vision before committing

1. What specific outcome does this serve? If unclear, name the anti-vision instead: what outcome is this trying to avoid?
2. Assume this fails. What caused it? Remove that cause now, before committing, not after.
3. What's actually known here versus assumed? What's the source?
4. What assets does this draw on: time, money, skills, network? What's missing?
5. Is there a runway? Does failure here destroy the baseline, or just cost the attempt?

If any answer is unclear, that's the work to do before starting, not after.
